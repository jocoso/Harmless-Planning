# Offices & Obstacles

*A browser-based text adventure exploring the intersection of interactive fiction, exploration, and the everyday office environment.*

## Overview

**Offices & Obstacles** is a browser-based text adventure that transforms the familiar office environment into a world of exploration and discovery.

Players take on the role of an office warrior, choosing from four distinct classes: **Manager, Technician, Marketing, and Accounting**. Each class representing a different perspective on navigating the workplace and uncovering its mysteries.

Rather than emphasizing traditional combat, the game focuses on exploration, environmental interaction, and narrative discovery. Players progress by examining their surroundings, interacting with the world, and engaging with text in an active and meaningful way and reading while doing so.

At its core, OfficesANDObstacles explores a simple idea: **what if reading became an adventure?**

## Key Features

- **Four Playable Classes:** Choose between Manager, Technician, Marketing, and Accounting.
- **Exploration-Focused Gameplay:** Discover the world through observation, investigation, and interaction.
- **Interactive Text:** Engage with written content as part of the gameplay experience.
- **Environmental Discovery:** Explore office spaces and uncover details hidden within the environment.
- **Narrative-Driven Experience:** Experience the world through descriptions, encounters, and discoveries.
- **Browser-Based Accessibility:** Experience the adventure directly through a web browser.

## Playable Classes

Each class offers a distinct identity within the office environment.

| Class | Focus | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| Manager | Leadership and organization | Navigate workplace dynamics and administrative challenges. |
| Technician | Technology and troubleshooting | Investigate systems, examine objects, and solve technical problems. |
| Marketing | Communication and creativity | Explore opportunities and approach situations through persuasion and observation. |
| Accounting | Analysis and attention to detail | Investigate inconsistencies, identify patterns, and follow clues. |


## Gameplay

Offices & Obstacles emphasizes exploration over combat.

The experience revolves around discovering the environment, interacting with its elements, and using written information to understand the world.

### Explore

Navigate the office environment and discover new locations, objects, and points of interest.

### Observe

Read environmental descriptions and pay attention to details that may reveal additional information.

### Interact

Engage with the world through text-based interactions and investigate the elements that capture your attention.

### Discover

Uncover details about the environment and experience the narrative through exploration.

## Design Philosophy

### Exploration Over Combat

Offices & Obstacles prioritizes curiosity and discovery rather than traditional combat mechanics. Progress is centered on exploring the environment and engaging with the world.

### Reading as Interaction

Text serves as more than a storytelling medium. It is an integral part of the gameplay experience.

Descriptions, narrative elements, and interactions encourage players to read actively, interpret information, and explore the possibilities presented by the game.

### The Ordinary as an Adventure

The office provides a familiar setting that can be reimagined as an environment full of unexpected encounters and discoveries.

By placing an adventure within an everyday workplace, the game creates an opportunity to explore the extraordinary through an ordinary perspective.
